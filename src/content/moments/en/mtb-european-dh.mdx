The UEC MTB Downhill European Championships came to Sofia for the first time, on the Laleto track at Vitoshko Lale on Vitosha. It was a dry, dusty weekend: every run threw up clouds that hung between the trees.

Conditions were hard on everyone, but the riders handled them brilliantly: fast, committed and still smiling at the bottom.
